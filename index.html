Pro tips

Get the best results

🎯
Be specific about your role

Don't just say "developer." Say "ML engineer who builds NLP pipelines." Specific roles produce better-targeted layouts and copy.

🎨
Match theme to audience

Applying for corporate roles? Go Editorial Minimal. Targeting startups? Dark Techy or Brutalist tends to stand out more.

⚡
Add your real project URLs

When you include live project links, the prompt tells the AI to make them clickable — so visitors can explore your work directly.

💬
Write a real bio

Two genuine sentences about what drives you produce far better About sections than a list of buzzwords. Authenticity is hard to replicate.
